Face Attendance System

Explore the powerful features designed to streamline your attendance process

🧠 AI-Powered Face Recognition
Uses advanced AI models to detect and verify student faces within milliseconds. No physical touch or manual entry is needed — attendance is marked instantly.
🔐 Dual Login Options
Login securely using either Face ID or a password. The system supports biometric verification for both users and administrators, preventing proxy attendance.
📊 Real-Time Attendance Reports
View and export attendance data in real time. Generate daily, weekly, or monthly reports for students, classes, or departments — all accessible in seconds.
📅 Smart Scheduling
Automatically syncs with academic calendars and class schedules. Alerts and notifications ensure attendance is tracked even on rescheduled sessions or holidays.
💾 Encrypted Data Storage
All attendance and facial data are encrypted and securely stored. Only authorized users can access or manage the database, ensuring student privacy and safety.
📱 Multi-Platform Compatibility
Accessible from desktops, tablets, and mobile devices. Optimized for modern web browsers and supports both light and dark modes based on device settings.
