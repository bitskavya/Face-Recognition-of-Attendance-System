📘 User Guide

1. Getting Started

Welcome to FaceAttend! This guide will help you navigate and use the application effectively.

  • Login with your registered credentials.
  • Navigate to Profile Settings to update your details.
  • Enable notifications to stay updated about attendance.

2. Profile Management

You can update your personal details under Edit Profile:

  • Change your name, email, phone number, and department.
  • Save changes to keep your records updated.

3. Notification Settings

Stay informed with notifications:

  • Email, SMS, Push notifications, and Reminders.
  • Toggle the options and click Save Settings.

4. Help & Support

If you face any issues:

  • 📘 Refer back to this User Guide.
  • 📞 Contact Support from the Help & Support page.
  • 💬 Check the FAQs for common queries.
